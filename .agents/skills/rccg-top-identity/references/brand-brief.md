# Brand Brief and Design History

## Confirmed
- Develop a modern, **distinctive parish identity** that clearly retains RCCG affiliation.
- Standalone logo text is **Tabernacle of Praise**.
- The official RCCG emblem stays independent and unchanged; side-by-side examples from other RCCG parishes illustrate **brand architecture only**, not aesthetic inspiration to copy.
- Visual personality: **modern, minimal but expressive**, elegant and welcoming.
- Colors: **controlled palette**, occasional justified exceptions; avoid overly colorful flyers.
- Photography: actual church imagery preferred; properly licensed conceptual photography allowed.
- Iterative review: flag significant unanswered decisions before moving to a new phase.

## Earlier exploration, not approval
- An early **Direction A architectural tabernacle** concept was liked for its sophisticated expression.
- Later Codex proposals `Open Tabernacle`, `Lifted Canopy` and `Gathered Arch` were exploratory. The Open Tabernacle was briefly selected as a refinement foundation only.
- Subsequent crude arch-and-stick-figure, split-top arch and doorway variations were **rejected** as generic and overly simplified.
- Additional user-provided church logo inspirations emphasize **crafted symbolism, negative space, line precision, inventive fusion of ideas, and considered typography**. They are quality references, not works to copy.
- An example combining harp, flame, and other worship imagery prompted a possible **harp/string or musical rhythm** motif for `Praise`. This is an experiment, **not a requirement**.

## Fresh opportunities
Explore the visual relationship between *Tabernacle* (sacred dwelling, sanctuary, gathering, welcome) and *Praise* (worship, thanksgiving, music, uplift) through one coherent, memorable form. Consider (not mandate): sanctuary without music; architecture + a subtle harp/lyre; abstract rhythmic negative space. Avoid stacking crosses, flames, doves, people and harps as separate clip-art.

Use distinct silhouettes, monochrome comparisons and actual-size tests. Pay attention to wordmark craft. Avoid default typography without a rationale.

## Not yet approved
- Final symbol/wordmark artwork
- Exact palette, HEX codes or typeface family
- Harp inclusion
- Official RCCG source artwork
- Combined lockup dimensions or official publishing templates

A navy / gold / ivory palette was a favorable early **candidate**; forest green and others are also exploratory.

**Do not present explorations as official brand standards.**
