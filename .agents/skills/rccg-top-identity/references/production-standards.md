# Creative Restrictions and Production Standards

## Do not
- Use rainbow/neon palettes, competing saturated colors, or overly colorful church flyers. Usually choose one dominant, one supporting and a restrained accent; photography and official RCCG artwork are separate.
- Confuse minimalism with bland pictograms or expressiveness with clutter.
- Repeat generic arch + person, house + cross, stock harp clip-art, or layered cross/flame/dove symbolism. Do not make concepts that differ only in color or tiny geometry.
- Copy other churches' protected logo artwork, distinctive silhouettes or promotional graphics. Inspiration should be analytical rather than imitative.
- Alter, stretch, recolor, redraw, crop, merge, generate or trace the official RCCG emblem. Use authorized artwork independently.
- Use excessive glows, fake 3D, gradients, typefaces, long paragraphs, crowded borders, illegible text or busy photography behind text.
- Fabricate event details, Scriptures, quotes, speakers, promises, testimonies, venues, contact details or church policies.
- Use unlicensed or watermarked images/fonts; misrepresent conceptual art as real RCCG TOP services. Preserve real people's appearance. Obtain relevant permissions, particularly for children.
- Publish, schedule, claim approval, or overwrite accepted designs without explicit authorization.

## Do
- First evaluate proposals with same-size **monochrome** samples, then color.
- Evaluate logo at small size, favicon/avatar, light/dark, horizontal/stacked, and separate RCCG pairing.
- Prefer real church photos with permission; licensed conceptual visuals can be used honestly.
- Maintain editable originals (SVG for marks) alongside social outputs.
- Test visual hierarchy and type legibility at normal phone sizes; for ordinary text, aim for 4.5:1 contrast, and large text 3:1 where applicable.
- Vary flyer composition by service without losing church recognition.
- Check license, source/credit where needed, permissions, and event facts.
- Request human sign-off before anything is treated as final or ready to publish.

## Weekly flyer checklist
1. Obtain verified title/theme, Scripture, speaker, date, time, venue, call-to-action, media and usage rights.
2. Use approved parish logo and officially supplied RCCG emblem; placeholder lockups are **internal drafts only**.
3. Create clear hierarchy, restrained palette, meaningful spacing and phone-safe margins.
4. Deliver appropriate platform formats (e.g., 1080 × 1350 Instagram feed; 1080 × 1920 story) and editable source.
5. Check accuracy and request approval before distributing or scheduling.

## Review state
For missing essentials, mark: `DRAFT — NOT FOR PUBLICATION. Needs: [specific missing details/assets/approval].`
Never claim trademark clearance or unique legal rights without appropriate checks.
