## Proposed exploration: how can architecture itself express praise?

In the existing S3 the changing heights of its folded wall segments create a visible cadence. Retain that formal energy as one worthwhile hypothesis, while questioning the exact original shapes.

Explore at least four **genuinely different** re-conceptions:
1. **Gathered courts** — a set of structurally joined surfaces encloses one distinctive open sacred interior; the movement of the folds expresses communal uplift.
2. **Praise as transformation** — adjacent dwelling surfaces gradually become more open or elevated without resembling a city skyline or signal-strength bars.
3. **Tabernacle material** — tensioned fabric, joined curtain elements, or veil-like folds produce rhythm without using an instrument, avoiding arbitrary straight-edged 'buildings'.
4. **Architectural counterform** — make the **negative space** between and within surfaces the central meaning; investigate an inviting threshold/room, not isolated facade pillars.

Creative freedom: propose another original direction if clearly stronger. Do not turn these into four near-identical five-column icons.

## What the logo must communicate

Tabernacle: dwelling/meeting/place of presence and gathering.
Praise: response, uplift, joy, worship, communal expression.
The same formal construction should make both meanings credible. An explanation should **deepen** a coherent identity, never rescue a random mark.
