## Rebuild and review limits

From this directory, `python3 tools/build_lockups.py` builds the outlined full-name SVGs and `python3 tools/build_delivery.py` builds the visible PNG/PDF review from those masters. Symbol-specific builders preserve meaningful versions; their folder notes describe exact native-size findings.

All artwork is monochrome. Geometry, spelling, export framing and native-size tests help establish usable draft delivery; they do not establish public recognition, originality clearance, physical fabrication quality or church approval. AGENTS.md and the RCCG identity skill remain unchanged. This is the two-option discussion checkpoint, not a final brand kit.
