# Open Praise — second contender study

**DRAFT — NOT FOR PUBLICATION.** A contender for comparison, not an approved church identity.

[View the optical and native-size proof](renders/optical-development.png).

The P-like black contour holds a negative T. Its open interior exits through the bottom of the drawing; this is one actual open contour, with no white overlay, masking, lettering font or raster in the mark. Tabernacle and Praise are linked through the letters, while the held opening can support an interpretation of welcome and response. The drawing does not literally show music, a congregation or worship.

This is a different recognition mechanism from Shared Rhythm: one compact letterform rather than a sequence of architectural planes. It follows candidate B on the [original ideation board](source/ideation-board.png); the other three candidates were not developed. The user-supplied reference logos inform confidence and figure–ground economy, rather than supplying their shield, book, figure, star or NV geometry.

Version 1 establishes the editable contour. Version 2 broadens the useful white crossbar and shortens its fragile curved hook. Both are preserved. Native 16/24/32/64 px black and reverse renders were inspected. The main opening holds; the hook softens at 16 px, so 24–32 px is the clearer letter-reading test. No final minimum-size rule is approved.

The two letters may not be equally obvious at first glance. A compact stylised monogram may suggest a publisher, creative studio or luxury brand; the full parish name will remain essential during recognition building. This is a candid limitation, not solved merely by explaining the symbolism. No trademark or legal clearance is claimed.

Run `python3 source/build.py` from this directory to rebuild the vectors. Run `python3 source/build_proofs.py` to rebuild native PNGs and the proof board using Inkscape, Pillow and the repository's licensed Noto Sans review font.
