CONCEPT DIRECTION

C / Praise Ligature

The current durability benchmark: a name-led shape with a broad footprint and fewer fine divisions. It remains the strongest practical starting point, while its editorial character and T/P separation need refinement.

B remains a valuable expressive alternative if music should lead the identity. Its strings need a genuine small-size solution, not brighter color.

D / RESONANT SANCTUARY — NEW

A folded surround defines shared space, opening into a rising rhythm. It deserves comparison because it brings dwelling and praise into the same structure. It also risks a leaf or growth reading.

Compare D against C in the next refinement round. D could take B’s place if sanctuary should lead over music; no replacement is approved, and D has not yet proved stronger overall.

Typography, final geometry, palette, and official two-logo arrangements remain open for your feedback.
