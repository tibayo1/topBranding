# Open Praise — Development 09

**DRAFT — NOT FOR PUBLICATION.** Continued development of the Review 08 second option, alongside Shared Rhythm. No new logo selection, palette, font or use rule is approved.

[Outline comparison](renders/outline-comparison.png) · [Enlarged pixel detail](renders/pixel-detail.png) · [Editable primary](primary.svg) · [Compact candidate](compact.svg)

The proposal keeps the **P-like outer contour and open negative T**. The full name must accompany it while the parish identity is unfamiliar. This is a name-based monogram, not a pictogram of praise.

The selected primary shortens the former long, beak-like white hook and replaces its tip with a small blunt terminal. The outer silhouette and flowing lower exit stay unchanged. The separate compact cut slightly widens the open vertical interval and reduces the counter terminal for raster use; it also makes the P more dominant. It is a candidate for comparison, not an approved substitute at a prescribed size.

Four meaningful states are preserved in `versions/`: the untouched Review 08 baseline, the selected balanced counter, a rejected level-terminal trial, and the compact cut. These are refinements of one concept, not four different concepts.

Actual 512 px and native 16/20/24/32/64 px black and reverse renders were inspected. The silhouette is more dependable than the letter puzzle at 16–20 px; 24–32 px affords clearer interior reading. The SVG audit found no structural issues, but does not assess whether the design is compelling, unique or approved.

The main unresolved risk remains unequal P/T recognition and a possible creative-studio, publisher or luxury association. No trademark clearance is claimed. The user references informed the principle of compact negative space; their shield, figure, book, star and NV geometry are not reused.

Run `python3 source/build.py` from any working directory to rebuild vectors, native raster proofs, metadata and comparison boards. Requires Python with Pillow and Inkscape. The mark uses no fonts, masks, clipping paths, overlays or external imagery. The proof labels use the repository's licensed Noto Sans; no label font is embedded in the mark.
