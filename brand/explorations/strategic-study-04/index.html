01 / Foundations

The name is the brief.

Tabernacle

Dwelling & belonging

Presence, sacred meeting, a place held together for worship.

×

Praise

Response & expression

Thanksgiving, joy, and people responding to God together.

Interpretive boundary

Biblical descriptions inform the ideas. The proposed visual metaphors are contemporary artistic interpretations, not an official parish theology or a reconstruction of the biblical tabernacle.

02 / Conceptual territories

Six ways into the name.

Distinct starting points, rather than variations of one icon. Each territory offers an idea to pursue and a risk to watch.

03 / Breadth before polish

First thoughts, made visible.

Initial vector studies at the same scale. These test different ideas and mark types; they are not finished identities. The selection notes explain what deserves development and what was set aside.

04 / Deeper development

Four ideas worth testing.

The shortlist prioritizes distinct ideas, a credible connection to the name, and the possibility of lasting recognition. No finalist is approved.
